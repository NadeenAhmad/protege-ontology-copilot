Ontology Copilot 0.9.31

A Protégé plugin that helps you draft and edit ontologies with an LLM assistant, using methods from peer-reviewed research (NeOn-GPT, Ontogenia, RIGOR-Lite and a direct draft). Every change is shown in Change Preview first; nothing changes until you press Apply.

New in 0.9.31: the default model is GPT-5.6 Luna through OpenRouter, and at every start a bar asks you to check your AI model; step 2 offers only the inputs the chosen method uses, with a box called CQs (one per line); Direct NL→OWL keeps running when you look at another step; Start over saves your ontology and opens a new, empty one.

Tested with Protégé 5.6.7 only. It may not work with other Protégé versions.

After installing, restart Protégé and click the Ontology Copilot tab (or Window > Tabs > Ontology Copilot). A bar at the top then asks you to set up your AI model: click Open Settings and add your OpenRouter key for the default model (GPT-5.6 Luna), or choose another provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini or a local Ollama, which needs no key).
