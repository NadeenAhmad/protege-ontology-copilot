Ontology Copilot 0.9.30

A Protégé plugin that helps you draft and edit ontologies with an LLM assistant, using methods from peer-reviewed research (NeOn-GPT, Ontogenia, RIGOR-Lite and a direct draft). Every change is shown in Change Preview first; nothing changes until you press Apply.

New in 0.9.30: the plugin starts with Gemini 3.7 Flash through OpenRouter, and until a model is set up, a bar at the top asks for it, with Open Settings right there. Picking another method than the recommended one shows that method's description and paper, and the tabs CQs and Manchester Syntax have clearer names.

Tested with Protégé 5.6.7 only. It may not work with other Protégé versions.

After installing, restart Protégé and click the Ontology Copilot tab (or Window > Tabs > Ontology Copilot). A bar at the top then asks you to set up your AI model: click Open Settings and add your OpenRouter key for the default model (Gemini 3.7 Flash), or choose another provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini or a local Ollama, which needs no key).
