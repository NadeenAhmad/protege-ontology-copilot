Ontology Copilot 0.9.32

A Protégé plugin that helps you draft and edit ontologies with an LLM assistant, using methods from peer-reviewed research (NeOn-GPT, Ontogenia, RIGOR-Lite and a direct draft). Every change is shown in Change Preview first; nothing changes until you press Apply.

New in 0.9.32: the chat starts with examples you can click, and What can I ask Ontology Copilot? below the box lists what it can and can't do; Enter sends. In a hurry, ask the chat for a first draft instead of going through the steps. The tutorial goes on inside Model & Settings, and the checks have one name each, under Ontology draft quality check.

Tested with Protégé 5.6.7 only. It may not work with other Protégé versions.

After the restart, click the Ontology Copilot tab (or Window > Tabs > Ontology Copilot). A bar at the top then asks you to set up your AI model: click Open Model & Settings and add your OpenRouter key for the default model (GPT-5.6 Luna), or choose another provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini or a local Ollama, which needs no key).
